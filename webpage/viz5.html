Visualization 5 — Event Heatmap

Global Events & BTC 3-Day Price Impact

A world map plotting major global events by geographic origin, where bubble size encodes the magnitude of BTC price change in the 3-day window following each event, fill color indicates price direction (teal = positive, red = negative), and border color identifies event type. The heavy concentration of bubbles over North America reflects the outsized influence of US-based regulatory and macroeconomic events on the Bitcoin market.
Global Events
Interaction: Hover over any country to see its dependence metrics and notable crypto-related events or policies.
Takeaway

From just observing the map, it can be seen that there is a concentration of world events that influence markets geographically – the majority of the bubbles appear above the US with a few in Europe and one in South America, while there are none in Asia, Africa, or the Middle East. This does not mean that these parts of the world do not have any crypto activities, but that the events that most strongly influenced the 3-day price of BTC occurred in the US. The combination of teal and red bubbles appearing in North America means that events originating in the US both positively and negatively affected the market with the biggest price changes.

Design Choices

The bubble map was selected over the bar graph or the table since geographic location is one of the fundamental factors in the dataset, and by positioning events on a world map, viewers can easily understand how many influential events occurred in one place, without having to refer to the name of the country. By using bubble sizes, impact can be shown in such a way that the largest bubbles are more influential, while the color inside and outside the bubble can represent whether the price goes up or down, and whether it is a market, government, or other type of event. The black world map was used not only for visual consistency but also to create high contrasts between the bright bubbles and the dark background, while the minimum impact slider allows viewers to concentrate solely on events with significant impacts, especially since there are many overlapping bubbles on the US.
